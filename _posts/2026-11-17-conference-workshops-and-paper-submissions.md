| **Venue / Track** | **Minimum Bar to Clear** | **Best Use Case** |
| **Top Conference Workshops** (e.g., NeurIPS/CVPR Workshops) | 4-page extended abstract; clear idea + preliminary proof-of-concept experiments. | Fast feedback, networking, and testing early-stage ideas before full scaling. |
| **TMLR (Transactions on Machine Learning Research)** | Correctness, clarity, and solid empirical/theoretical rigor—**explicitly does not reject for "low perceived novelty."** | Methodical studies, negative results, reproducibility audits, and clean incremental improvements. |
| **Main Conference Tracks** (NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR) | Complete 8–9 page narrative, strong recent baselines, thorough ablations, and clear novelty/significance. | Mature projects ready to defend against 3–4 critical reviewers. |
| **Position / Datasets & Benchmarks Tracks** | Deep literature synthesis with a concrete call to action (Position) or high-quality open-source data + baseline suite (Benchmarks). | Conceptual critiques, ethics/policy frameworks, or new evaluation suites. |
