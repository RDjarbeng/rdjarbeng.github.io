## How Starlink works, in brief

Starlink satellites fly in low Earth orbit. [Wikipedia's Starlink article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starlink) says SpaceX chose a relatively low altitude of about 550 km. Wikipedia notes that this low orbit gives Starlink lower latency than traditional geostationary satellite internet.

The user terminal is a flat dish about the size of a pizza box. It uses a phased-array antenna to track satellites as they move across the sky. The Branch Education video animates this beamforming in detail.

The satellites also talk to each other. Starlink satellites carry laser links that pass data between them in space. Wikipedia notes these inter-satellite laser links were first tested successfully in late 2020. Wikipedia gives Antarctica as one example, where satellites with these links connect users to distant ground stations.

## Starlink in Africa and Rwanda

Starlink reached Africa in early 2023. [TeleGeography's table](https://resources.telegeography.com/starlink-expanding-in-africa) lists Nigeria first, in January 2023, and Rwanda second, in February 2023. By January 2025 the same table lists 17 African countries, including Kenya, Mozambique, Malawi, Zambia, Ghana and Zimbabwe.

In Rwanda, the service launched officially on 22 February 2023 at the Kigali Convention Centre. [KT Press](https://www.ktpress.rw/2023/02/elon-musks-starlink-officially-begins-operations-in-rwanda/) reported that Rwanda was the first country in the region to offer Starlink. It gave the hardware price as RWF 572,000, a one-time purchase, and the service at RWF 48,000 a month. It also reported that the government had picked 500 remote schools to receive Starlink terminals.

South Africa did not have the service. TeleGeography links this mainly to the country's Black Economic Empowerment ownership rules for telecom operators.

**Update (November 2026):** TeleGeography counted 23 African countries with live Starlink service by October 2025, and South Africa was still not among them. Wikipedia reports that Starlink became available to consumers in Uganda on 1 September 2026.
