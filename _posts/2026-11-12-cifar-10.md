The [CIFAR-10 Dataset](https://github.com/RubixML/CIFAR-10) is **a foundational computer vision collection created by Alex Krizhevsky, Vinod Nair, and Geoffrey Hinton to benchmark machine learning and image recognition algorithms**. \[[1](https://www.kaggle.com/c/cifar-10), [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIFAR-10)]

Dataset Overview

- **Total Images:** 60,000 color images
- **Resolution:** 32 × 32 pixels (3 channels: Red, Green, Blue)
- **Classes:** 10 distinct object categories (6,000 images per class)
- **Split:** 50,000 training images and 10,000 test images
- **Balance:** Perfectly balanced across all classes (5,000 training and 1,000 testing per class) \[[1](https://cave.cs.toronto.edu/kriz/cifar.html), [2](https://colab.research.google.com/github/pytorch/tutorials/blob/gh-pages/_downloads/cifar10_tutorial.ipynb), [3](https://docs.ultralytics.com/datasets/classify/cifar10)]

The 10 Classes

1. Airplane
2. Automobile / Car
3. Bird
4. Cat
5. Deer
6. Dog
7. Frog
8. Horse
9. Ship
10. Truck \[[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIFAR-10), [2](https://colab.research.google.com/github/pytorch/tutorials/blob/gh-pages/_downloads/cifar10_tutorial.ipynb)]

Common Use Cases

- **Model Benchmarking:** Frequently used to test new convolutional neural network (CNN) architectures, regularization methods, and training acceleration techniques. \[[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIFAR-10)]
- **Accessibility:** Because the images are low-resolution (32 × 32), researchers and students can prototype and train models quickly on standard hardware or single GPUs. \[[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIFAR-10), [2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fLBbyhPbWzY)]
- **Framework Integration:** Readily available through popular deep learning libraries like [PyTorch Torchvision](https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/main/generated/torchvision.datasets.CIFAR10.html), [TensorFlow Datasets](https://www.tensorflow.org/datasets/catalog/cifar10), and [Hugging Face Datasets](https://huggingface.co/datasets/uoft-cs/cifar10). \[[1](https://huggingface.co/datasets/uoft-cs/cifar10), [2](https://www.tensorflow.org/datasets/catalog/cifar10), [3](https://docs.pytorch.org/vision/main/generated/torchvision.datasets.CIFAR10.html)]
