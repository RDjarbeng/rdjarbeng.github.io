- With QuickEdit on, clicking or dragging in the window starts **selecting text to copy**, and the title changes to “Select …”.
- While text is selected, Windows **freezes the window’s output** so the text doesn’t scroll away before you copy it.
- The catch is that the program running inside can’t print anything while that’s happening. As soon as it tries, which a progress counter does constantly, it gets stuck waiting. So a stray click pauses the whole program, not just the display.
- Pressing **Esc**, **Enter** or right-click ends the selection and it carries on.
