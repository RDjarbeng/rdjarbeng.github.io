The AI quota is a free API credit allowance provided by Kaggle for Kaggle Benchmarks (and its Model Proxy). It is completely separate from your standard weekly notebook compute hours.   1. AI Quota ($10.00 Daily / $100.00 Monthly)What it pays for: Calling hosted frontier LLMs (such as Gemini, Claude, and GPT models) via Kaggle's Model Proxy and the kaggle-benchmarks SDK.   How it works: Instead of bringing your own paid API keys to evaluate or benchmark LLMs on custom tasks, Kaggle covers the inference token costs up to $10 per day and $100 per month at no cost to you.   When it deducts: Every time you run a Benchmark task, evaluate a model, or use an LLM-as-a-judge through Kaggle's proxy, the token cost is calculated based on the specific model's pricing tier and subtracted from both your daily and monthly balances. (Note: Certain AI-focused hackathons or competitions occasionally grant temporary higher quotas, such as $50/day.)
