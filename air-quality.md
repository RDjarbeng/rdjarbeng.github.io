At Carnegie Mellon University Africa I worked on the design of low-cost weather and air quality sensors deployed across East Africa, and most of what I write about the environment comes from that work. This page collects those posts in a sensible reading order: start with the basics, then the standards that define "safe", then where to find data, then what it takes to measure air in the field.

Last reviewed: 20 November 2026. See the [editorial policy](/editorial-policy/) for how posts are sourced and corrected.
