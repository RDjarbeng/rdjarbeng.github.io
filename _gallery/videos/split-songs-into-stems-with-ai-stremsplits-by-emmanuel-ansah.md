Want to split vocals and instruments out of a song fast? We tried a new app by Emmanuel Ansah that pulls stems from any track in under 30 seconds.

Try it yourself: [stemsplits.com](https://stemsplits.com)

It runs right in your browser, no installs. Good for grabbing an acapella, building a mashup, or putting together a karaoke track.

**Credits**
App developer: [Emmanuel Ansah](https://www.linkedin.com/in/emmanuel-ansah-9b193a216/)
Tested by: RDjarbeng for [rdjarbeng.com](https://rdjarbeng.com)

To install this app as an android app (iOS not included yet) as shown in the demo follow these steps:

 • Join this group: https://groups.google.com/g/song-splitter-testers

 • Open this on your Android phone: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.stemsplits.app

 • Tap Become a tester and install it from Google Play.

 • Split a song or two, then keep the app installed for 2 weeks.

That's it! It's completely free, has no ads, and there's nothing to pay.
