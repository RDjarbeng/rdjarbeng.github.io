On November 20, 2026, Levant Alpoge announced a proof of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, predominantly generated by AI. It is 1.3GB in lean, and Alpoge estimates that it would exceed 250k pages if translated to natural language.



Shortly thereafter, Alessandro Mascullo posted about his own (purely human) proposed proof. He was planning for much more time to have it reviewed, but given the circumstances, posted the partial draft.
