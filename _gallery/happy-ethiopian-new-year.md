**Additional comments:**

The Ethiopian calendar offers a beautiful and unique perspective on time, celebrating the start of a new year in September. This season marks the end of the long rainy season and the arrival of bright sunshine, which is perfectly captured by the blooming yellow daisies known as Adey Abeba. It is a time for renewal, gratitude, and gathering with loved ones to embrace the traditions that make this culture so vibrant. Wishing everyone a joyous and peaceful transition into the new year.
