NASA astronauts Reid Wiseman, Victor Glover, Christina Koch, and CSA astronaut Jeremy Hansen participate in a crew question-and-answer session ahead of the Artemis II lunar flyby mission. The session included details on their zero-gravity indicator, a longstanding spaceflight tradition where a small tethered object is placed in the cabin to signal when the spacecraft has achieved weightlessness after trans-lunar injection.
