**Additional comments:**

Michael Jackson redefined the spectacle of the Super Bowl halftime show on January 31, 1993, at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena, California. This iconic performance shifted the entire trajectory of live entertainment by introducing high production value and massive scale to the event. Jackson stood motionless for nearly two minutes at the start of his set, commanding the attention of millions of viewers before breaking into his signature high energy choreography. His ensemble, featuring a striking black military style jacket adorned with intricate gold detailing, remains one of the most recognizable outfits in music history. This legendary appearance solidified his status as the King of Pop and set a permanent standard for every halftime performer who followed in his footsteps.
