What fish is this? Strange things to do with AI

My professor from Ghana took this photo. And I had one question, what kind of fish is that. Instead of standing around wondering I just thought I'd ask one of these AI tools. 

According to gemini:

That is a Barracuda (genus Sphyraena), most likely a Great Barracuda (Sphyraena barracuda) or a Guinean Barracuda (Sphyraena afra), which is common along the West African coast.
Key Identifying Features
 * Elongated Profile: A long, slender, torpedo-shaped body built for fast bursts of speed.
 * Distinct Head & Jaw: A pointed snout with a prominent lower jaw that extends noticeably beyond the upper jaw (an underbite).
 * Teeth: Large, razor-sharp, fang-like teeth set inside a wide gape.
 * Coloration: Dark bluish-gray to greenish back fading to a bright silver belly, often with faint dark vertical crossbars or blotches along the upper flanks.
