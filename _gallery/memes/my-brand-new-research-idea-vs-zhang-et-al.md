**Additional comments:**

Every dedicated researcher knows the specific panic of discovering their groundbreaking hypothesis has already been documented by someone else. You spend weeks pouring over data and refining your methodology, only to find that Zhang et al have already paved that path years ago. It feels as though there is a mystical force in the academic community ensuring that no idea is truly original before it hits the journal presses. This meme perfectly captures the relentless nature of academic literature searches, where the common surname Zhang appears with uncanny frequency across every conceivable field of study. Whether you are working on advanced physics, biology, or computer science, you can bet your tenure that a paper by Zhang et al is waiting in the wings to disrupt your grand plans. It serves as a humorous reminder to stay humble and always double check your citations because the competition is vast, prolific, and seemingly everywhere at once.
