Richard is a software and machine learning engineer with an MSc in Electrical and Computer Engineering from Carnegie Mellon University. Since October 2026 he has been a Research Associate at the Kigali Collaborative Research Center. Before that, as a Research Associate at Carnegie Mellon University Africa (2024 to 2025), he built and deployed low-cost weather sensors across East Africa. He writes about machine learning, computer vision, IoT, satellite and climate data, and life in Kigali.
